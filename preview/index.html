Junior coaching, 6–18

A clear pathway: start in Beginners and move up as your child's skills grow. Our aim is for juniors to enjoy the game and develop the skills to play competitively in local leagues.

  1. BeginnersFridays 5:30–7pm

    New to badminton. Learn the basics in a fun, friendly group.

  2. IntermediateSaturdays 5–7pm

    For players who can already rally. Two hours of coaching.

  3. AdvancedSaturdays 3:30–5pm

    For experienced juniors. Strict assessment and invitation only.
