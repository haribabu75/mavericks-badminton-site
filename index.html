Membership Terms

  • Monthly rolling contract: no discounts or refunds for absences, and fees are not carried forward for a 'no show'.
  • Pre-payment by monthly BACS only (standing orders accepted).
  • Membership fee must be paid, and any break notified, by the 25th of the previous month.
  • 3, 4 or 5 sessions in a calendar month incur the same membership fee.
  • The Academy books the courts and provides the shuttles. All players and coaches are Badminton England members, which includes Badminton England’s liability insurance. The Academy is not responsible for venue-cancelled bookings but deals fairly with refunds.
  • All communication is through the Mavericks Academy WhatsApp groups.
